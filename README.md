# 🚀 MarketPilot AI

> **Analyze. Understand. Invest.**

MarketPilot AI is an AI-powered investment intelligence platform that helps everyday investors discover promising opportunities across **Crypto**, **Stocks**, and **ETFs** through transparent, easy-to-understand analysis.

---

## 🌟 Vision

Make investment research simple, transparent, and accessible to everyone.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Help investors make smarter decisions in less than one minute.
