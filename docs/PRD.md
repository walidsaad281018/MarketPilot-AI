# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# MarketPilot AI

Version: 0.1 Alpha

Author: Walid Saad & Atlas (OpenAI)

Status: In Development

---

# 1. Vision

MarketPilot AI is an AI-powered investment intelligence platform that helps investors identify promising opportunities across Crypto, Stocks, and ETFs using transparent, explainable analysis.

The platform is designed to simplify investment research while maintaining transparency and user trust.

---

# 2. Mission

Analyze.

Understand.

Invest.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Most investment platforms overwhelm users with excessive data.

MarketPilot AI filters market information and presents only the most relevant investment opportunities with clear explanations.

---

# 4. Target Users

• Beginner Investors

• Intermediate Investors

• Busy Professionals

• Pi Network Community

---

# 5. Supported Markets

- Cryptocurrency
- Stocks
- ETFs

---

# 6. MVP Features

## Dashboard

Displays today's best investment opportunities.

---

## Opportunity Center

Top 20 Crypto

Top 20 Stocks

Top 20 ETFs

---

## AI Analysis

Every opportunity includes:

- AI Score
- Confidence
- Risk Level
- Three Reasons
- Technical Indicators

---

## Transparency Center

Tracks historical recommendations and success rates.

---

## Watchlist

Allows users to save favorite assets.

---

# 7. AI Principles

The AI must:

- Explain every recommendation.
- Never guarantee profits.
- Display confidence levels.
- Show risk levels.
- Maintain transparency.

---

# 8. Design Principles

- Simplicity
- Trust
- Speed
- Transparency

---

# 9. Success Metrics

The MVP is successful when a user can:

- Discover an opportunity
- Understand why it was selected
- Save it
- Return the next day
